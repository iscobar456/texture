Delete your account

You can delete your Texture account and all of its data at any time. It cannot be undone.

In the app

Phone (Android or iPhone)

  1. Tap the account icon in the top right corner.
  2. Choose Delete account…
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete account.

Desktop

  1. Open Settings (Ctrl+, or Cmd+,).
  2. Under Account, choose Delete account…
  3. Enter your password and choose Delete account.

Export any PDFs you want to keep first.

By email

If you can't use the apps, email support@texture.isaacspencer.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. For your protection, requests from any other address need extra confirmation. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and password.
  • All documents, folders, templates, snippets and synced settings, including deleted documents that could still be restored.
  • All photos in your inbox, used and trash lists, with their transcriptions.
  • All images and page previews stored for your documents.

Deletion from the app takes effect immediately. Copies may remain in the hosting provider's backups and logs until they expire on their normal schedule. On the computer you delete from, the local copy is removed too; other computers keep the files they already had until you remove the app or its data there.
