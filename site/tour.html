Tour

A look at the desktop and phone apps. Click any screenshot to see it full size.

Write with a live preview

Type LaTeX on the left and see the PDF on the right. Texture compiles when you pause, points you to the line of any error, and exports the finished PDF. It runs offline, includes its own LaTeX engine, and has autocomplete you define yourself, an optional Vim mode, and several color themes.

Send work from your phone

Photograph your paper or whiteboard, rotate and crop it, and send. It shows up in the desktop's photo inbox within seconds while the app is open (or the next time you open it), and the phone marks it Delivered. Drag a photo into your document to insert it as a figure.

Turn handwriting into LaTeX

Transcribe a photo and review the LaTeX next to the original before anything reaches your document. It uses your own Anthropic API key, so you pay only for what you use, and each transcription is saved so reopening it is free.

Organized, and backed up

Keep documents in folders, start them from your own templates, and see each one's first page at a glance. Everything is saved on your computer and backed up to your account, so a new computer gets it all back, and deleted documents can be restored for 30 days.
