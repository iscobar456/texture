Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

This policy covers the Texture desktop app (Linux, macOS, Windows), the Texture phone app (Android, iPhone, and its web version), and this website. Texture is made by Isaac Spencer, an individual developer ("I", "me"). If you have a question about it, write to support@texture.isaacspencer.com.

In short: Texture collects what it needs to keep your account and your homework, nothing more. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold.

What is collected, and why

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create and sign in to your account, confirm your email and reset your password. The password is stored only in hashed form by the authentication service; I cannot see it.
Your documents: the LaTeX text, document and folder names, templates, your snippets file, and the images inserted into documentsBacked up to your account so you can restore everything on a new device.
Page previews: a picture of the first page of each document and templateTo show thumbnails in the document picker on every device.
Photos you send from the phone, after your rotate and cropTo deliver them to the photo inbox in the desktop app. Only the photos you choose to send are uploaded; the app never reads the rest of your photo library or camera roll.
Transcriptions: the LaTeX text produced from a photo, including your editsSaved with the photo so you are not charged twice to transcribe it again.
A few settings: your custom transcription instructions and your default templateSo they follow your account to a new computer. Other settings (theme, font size and so on) stay on each device.
Technical records: sign-in times, IP address, and request logs kept by the hosting providerTo run and secure the service (for example, to stop repeated password guessing). They are not used to profile you.

Texture does not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers or device identifiers, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.

What stays on your device

  • Your Anthropic API key, if you add one for transcription. It is stored only on that computer and is never uploaded to Texture's servers or synced.
  • The desktop app's log file, used for troubleshooting. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to me.
  • The phone's list of sent photos (the last 50, with their copies), so you can see what was delivered.
  • Thumbnails, your interface settings, and the working copy of each document.

Camera and photo library

The phone app asks for the camera when you choose to take a photo, and for your photo library when you choose to pick one. It uses them only for that photo. Rotating and cropping happen on the phone before anything is sent.

Who processes your data

Texture uses these service providers. Each receives only what it needs for its task.

Your data is not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long data is kept

  • Your account and its contents are kept until you delete them or delete the account.
  • A deleted document can be restored for 30 days. Its images and preview are then removed from storage. Its text stays on the server, hidden, so your other devices learn that it was deleted, until you delete your account.
  • Photos moved to the inbox's trash are permanently deleted after 7 days.
  • Deleting your account removes it and everything in it immediately. Copies may remain in the providers' backups and logs until those expire on their normal schedule.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data from inside either app, or by email. See Delete your account.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each account can only read its own rows and files: this is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), and stored files are private. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care to protect your data.

Your rights

You can see and change your documents in the app, export them as PDF, and delete them or your whole account at any time. You may also ask me for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to support@texture.isaacspencer.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. I will answer within 30 days.

Where the GDPR applies, your data is processed to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract) and, for security records, for the legitimate interest of keeping the service safe. Your data is stored in the United States.

Children

Texture is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact me and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
