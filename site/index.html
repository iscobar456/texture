Texture

Write homework in LaTeX on your computer. Add photos of your paper or whiteboard work from your phone.

How it works

  1. Write your assignment in the desktop app, with a live PDF preview.
  2. Photograph handwritten work with the phone app. It lands in the desktop's photo inbox.
  3. Insert the photo as a figure, or transcribe it to LaTeX with your own Anthropic API key, then export the PDF.
