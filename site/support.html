Support

Questions, problems or feedback: write to support@texture.isaacspencer.com. I usually answer within a few days.

Bug reports and feature requests are also welcome as GitHub issues.

Common questions

My photo did not arrive on the computer

Check that both apps are signed in to the same account and that the desktop app is open. Photos sent while it is closed appear in its inbox the next time it opens. The phone shows "Delivered" once the desktop has shown a photo.

The first compile takes a long time

The first time you compile on a computer, Texture downloads the LaTeX fonts and packages it needs, which takes about a minute and needs an internet connection. After that, compiling works offline.

How do I set up transcription?

Transcribing a photo to LaTeX uses your own Anthropic API key. Create one at console.anthropic.com, then add it in the desktop app under Settings → Photo OCR. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.

I forgot my password

Choose "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. Open the emailed link on a phone with the Texture app installed: the app opens and asks for a new password.

I did not get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder, then use "Resend email" on the check-your-email screen. Emails come from texture.isaacspencer.com.

Moving to a new computer

Install Texture and sign in. Your documents, templates, snippets and photos are restored from your account. The transcription API key is not synced, so add it again.

macOS says the app "can't be opened" or "is damaged"

Older downloads were not notarized by Apple. Move Texture to Applications and open it once. On macOS 15 or later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose "Open Anyway" next to Texture. On older versions, right-click Texture, choose Open, then Open again.
