        About Texture
        
        
      

      

        
Texture

        
Version 

        
Write homework in LaTeX on your computer and pull in photos of paper and whiteboard work from your phone, then export a PDF.

        
    
          
  • Documents and templates are stored on this device and sync to your account, so everything works offline.
    • 
          
  • Photos you take in the Texture phone app arrive in the photo inbox. Insert them as images, or transcribe them to LaTeX with your own Anthropic API key.
    • 
          
  • Press Ctrl/Cmd+P to jump between documents.
